01 / Scientific boundary

Pharmaceutical mediation, not electrical therapy in general.

Inclusion requires evidence that electricity changes a medicinal or bioactive molecular process. Wired and wireless approaches are eligible. A generic electrical cancer treatment, ablation, neuromodulation or tissue stimulation does not qualify simply because it uses electricity.

Drug action

Electrochemotherapy and uptake

Electrical permeabilisation and field-mediated changes in drug uptake, sensitivity, resistance and intracellular trafficking.

Delivery

Controlled molecular release

Electrochemical pumps, conductive-polymer release, iontronics and electrically triggered delivery of active compounds.

Molecular actuation

Electrochemical and nano-bioelectronic medicines

Electrically controlled redox, bioactive nanomaterials and molecular signalling linked to a therapeutic endpoint.

Boundary: TTFields combined with chemotherapy is not, by itself, evidence that the field mediates drug action. Pure device engineering, impedance sensing, transmembrane transport mechanisms and unmedicated cell stimulation remain visible as supporting context but are not automatically scored as pharmaceutical interventions.

04 / Methods & uncertainty

Evidence should precede position.

01 · Retrieve

DOIs and source records

Harvest OpenAlex and Europe PMC; preserve search strings and query errors; reconcile published DOI records without inventing missing publications.

02 · Screen

Separate molecular mechanism

Check that electrical input changes a pharmaceutical or therapeutic molecular response. Keep diagnostics, platforms, reviews and unmediated electrical treatment separate.

03 · Verify

Independently adjudicate

Confirm author identities, original research status, full-text mechanistic inclusion and field-specific retrieval performance before trusting positions.

What does the current score mean?

The provisional index retains the Atlas's exploratory components: 35% citation standing within this harvested cohort, 25% publication output, 20% authorship position, 10% title-indicated translation, and 10% years active. These weights are not independently validated or externally normalised for this scientific field. They cannot establish an international league table.

What is excluded from scoring?

Standalone tumour-treating fields, cardiac pulsed-field ablation, generic electrical wound care and neurostimulation; reviews, diagnostics, preprints and unverified journal status; and pure technology or electron-transfer studies lacking demonstrated pharmaceutical or molecular therapeutic effects. Such work may still be displayed as supporting context.

How do I report an omitted paper or incorrect identity?

Include the DOI, publisher page, the researcher’s author identifier and which pharmaceutical action is electrically mediated. Missing works and disputed identities should be resolved for every researcher using the same criteria.
