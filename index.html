01 / Research boundaries

Wireless describes delivery, not mechanism.

Energy modality, transduction mechanism, therapeutic endpoint and experimental evidence are classified independently. A treatment triggered by ultrasound, for example, is not automatically an electrochemical therapy.

03 / Reproducible research

Methodology and quality controls.

  1. Discovery: Run documented queries through Europe PMC (including PubMed) and OpenAlex; retain exact search strings, date, pagination and failures.
  2. Deduplication: Merge DOI, PMID and title–year matches, preserve source identifiers, and review ambiguous matches.
  3. Eligibility: Two-stage abstract and full-text screening; separate original therapy evidence from reviews, diagnostics and enabling mechanisms.
  4. Author identities: Check ORCID, institution, OpenAlex identifiers and original paper bylines. Unresolved names remain unranked.
  5. Impact: Assess relevant original research, field- and year-normalised citations, authorship contribution, therapeutic translation and sustained influence; publish sensitivity tests.
  6. Ranking release: Record denominators, exclusions, missingness, uncertainty and independent verification before displaying numeric positions.

Automated candidate discovery is not a complete or validated census. Page limits, access restrictions and review requirements are recorded explicitly.
