# Electro-Mediated Pharmaceutics — prospective evidence and comparison protocol

**Version: October 2026. Scientific status: not independently validated.**

## Review question

Which independently identifiable original research studies demonstrate **electrically or electrochemically mediated pharmaceutical or therapeutic molecular action**, and which verified researchers contributed?

This is **not** a comparison of all electrical treatments or all remotely actuated therapies.

## Core eligibility

A full-text reviewer must be able to identify:
1. Original experimental journal work, dated 2000–2026, with traceable version of record.
2. Defined electrical or electrochemical input: applied field, pulse, current, interface/electrode, bipolar polarization or conductive molecular actuator; wired systems may qualify.
3. A medicinal agent, bioactive molecule or pharmaceutical molecular mechanism.
4. Experimental evidence that electrical action changes the molecule's release, drug uptake, intracellular handling, bioavailability, chemical/redox activity, drug sensitivity, delivery or therapeutic molecular effect.
5. Enough information to judge causation and the strength of evidence, including appropriate electric/no-electric and medicinal/no-medicinal controls.

Simply combining TTFields with a chemotherapeutic drug does not prove electrical mediation of that drug's action.

## Excluded from researcher-scoring; retained as context when relevant

- Standalone TTFields, electrical cancer therapy, irreversible ablation, cardiac pulsed-field ablation, electric wound care and non-pharmaceutical DBS/VNS/SCS/tDCS.
- Sensing, impedance, electrode fabrication, material synthesis, pure transport theory and membrane redox mechanisms without demonstrated pharmaceutical effect.
- Reviews, editorials, conference abstracts, data descriptors, journal-cover art, preprints/repository duplicate versions, and articles without reliably identified journal source.
- Ultrasound/magnetic/optical/RF therapy with no electrical mediation of the pharmaceutical process.

## Retrieval and field completeness

Use documented OpenAlex and Europe PMC query families, exact DOI source checks, publisher pages, ORCID exports and institutionally sourced research biographies. Preserve query timestamps, database errors, page limits, deduplication, missing records and all exclusion reasons. Perform independent citation-chasing and a field-specific precision/recall pilot; a DOI sentinel passing retrieval checks is not evidence of an exhaustive scientific search.

## Screen and validate

Two independent reviewers assess the title/abstract then full text, classify direct pharmaceutical intervention versus mechanistic context versus unrelated electrical treatment, and record evidence strength. Disagreements require an explicit adjudication trail. The clinical pharmaceutical effect must be distinguished from mere device function and from unrelated electrical killing of cells.

At the work level, check the publisher byline plus ORCID and OpenAlex author ID; name-only matches cannot establish identity. Record the dated institute for each academic, not an unverified current employer. Reconcile all researchers symmetrically.

The current publisher-verified Rawson six-DOI reference sample is intentionally incomplete. Its results cannot substantiate a complete researcher publication count or a scientific position.

## Comparison and release

Machine candidate scores are exploratory and currently use 35% internal citation percentiles, 25% relevant output, 20% first/last authorship, 10% title-level translation indicators, and 10% years active. These components are not yet scientifically validated. Full-text screening, independent bibliography completeness, field/year normalization, author identity adjudication and sensitivity analysis must precede publication of defensible worldwide numerical standings.

**Stop criterion: never describe a source-relative candidate ranking as a validated league table.**
